# Product and scope
Learners scan handwritten notes or upload PDFs and turn scattered resources into a learning path, grounded tutor, quizzes, and next-step guidance. The idea originated with Temitope; this package organizes its implementation.

## Demo outcome
Upload → extract → concepts → learning path → tutor → quiz → evidence → recommended revision.
Demonstrate one full loop and one second-domain fixture to show domain independence.

## Must have
Image/PDF ingestion, extraction review, concept dependencies, ordered path, cited tutor responses, concept-tagged MCQs, evidence-based progress, and remedial recommendation.

## Constraints
Text-first mobile UI; keyboard access; readable contrast; uncertainty surfaced; private materials; source traceability. Cache a selected study pack for low bandwidth. New AI responses require connectivity.

## Study spaces and materials
A study space represents a learner's goal (exam preparation, learning a topic, or studying a course), not a single uploaded document or a fixed school-subject taxonomy. Learners can create multiple spaces, leave optional context such as subject, level, and exam date, and add documents over time. Each material is classified by its role (for example study notes, syllabus, past paper, or marking scheme). Exam-preparation suggestions are optional prompts, never upload requirements. Concepts and the study path combine evidence from all materials in the same space while retaining per-material source references.

## Defer
Billing, voice, YouTube ingestion, opportunity matching, complex roles, comprehensive multilingual generation, and sophisticated mastery algorithms.
