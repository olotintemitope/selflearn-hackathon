- [x] Allow the owner to submit ready candidates, keep needs-review candidates out of practice, hide detected responses until submission, and save an owner-scoped attempt with source page and answer basis.

## Out of scope
Scientifically validated mastery.

## Verification
Test deterministic behavior and failures with provider fakes; exercise the visible flow and record evidence. Feature tests travel beyond each server deadline, submit otherwise-correct answers, and verify that no late answer is scored and the result is finalized as unanswered. Expired past-paper sessions also finalize when reopened.

## Current limitation
The imported bank supports single-paper and mixed-paper practice from current clear questions, but it is not an official, proctored, or fully marked WAEC simulation. The learner selects a 30, 60, or 90 minute practice timer; it does not claim to reflect official paper timing. Mixed sets accept papers from one owned study space and preserve each question's source paper, year hint, and page. Paper years are upload-label hints, not official verification. Detected marks are not summed or used for scoring. Owner-checked candidates can be corrected and practised, but are always ungraded until answer-key verification is implemented. Only untouched clear objective answer labels are scored, as an unverified comparison. A learner-confirmed answer-key-to-paper association now records the exact paper and answer-key materials and their extraction versions; it does not establish any individual answer. Per-question answer matching and confirmation remain open, and no answer is inferred from title, order, or proximity. Written and fill-gap answers remain ungraded. Official timing association and imported-question worked solutions remain open. Marks are kept unknown when extraction is ambiguous. A live browser run on a supplied one-page Physics scan verified Mathpix extraction, 12 indexed MCQs, 11 practice-ready questions, formula rendering, and submitted results; the OCR-missing option in question 4 remained held for owner review. All 11 submitted responses were saved without a score because the paper has no verified answer key. A mixed-paper live run is still needed. The concept-level exam generates original single-answer, four-option MCQs and one AI-marked written response from concept-linked study chunks; its 20-minute timer is not a full-paper simulation or proctored exam. Latest-attempt points and the 70% review prompt are planning heuristics, not validated mastery.
