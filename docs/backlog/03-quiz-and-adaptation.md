Expired past-paper sessions also finalize when reopened. This slice has no live provider, real scanned-paper, or browser acceptance evidence.

## Current limitation
The imported bank supports single-paper and mixed-paper practice from current clear questions, but it is not an official, proctored, or fully marked WAEC simulation. The learner selects a 30, 60, or 90 minute practice timer; it does not claim to reflect official paper timing. Mixed sets accept papers from one owned study space and preserve each question's source paper, year hint, and page. Paper years are upload-label hints, not official verification. Detected marks are not summed or used for scoring. Owner-checked candidates can be corrected and practised, but are always ungraded until answer-key verification is implemented. Only untouched clear objective answer labels are scored, as an unverified comparison. Candidate-level submitted written and fill-gap responses can receive qualitative, source-grounded step feedback with a separate verbatim source excerpt, but they remain unscored; paper-set and mixed-paper results do not yet receive this feedback. Marking-scheme matching, official timing association, and full imported-question worked-solution coverage remain open. Marks are kept unknown when extraction is ambiguous. A prior live browser run on a supplied one-page Physics scan verified the existing extraction, indexing, and practice flow: 12 indexed MCQs, 11 practice-ready questions, and submitted results; the OCR-missing option in question 4 remained held for owner review. All 11 submitted responses were saved without a score because the paper had no verified answer key. That run predates the candidate-level feedback change and does not verify it. A mixed-paper live run is still needed. The concept-level exam generates original single-answer, four-option MCQs and one AI-marked written response from concept-linked study chunks; its 20-minute timer is not a full-paper simulation or proctored exam. Latest-attempt points and the 70% review prompt are planning heuristics, not validated mastery.
